RUHI Browser Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Publisher: RUHI Applications (Developer: Saikh Sufian) • Supported Platforms: Windows 10/11 • macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon & Intel) • Android 6.0+

At RUHI Applications, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right, not an optional preference. RUHI Browser was engineered from its very foundation with an uncompromising Zero-Telemetry Architecture. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data handling principles, local hardware encryption standards, and complete absence of tracking across all supported platforms: Microsoft Windows, Apple macOS, and Google Android.

1. Zero Remote Telemetry & Absolute Privacy

RUHI Browser does NOT harvest, monitor, intercept, store, sell, or transmit your personal data, web activity, or search queries to RUHI Applications or any third-party marketing brokers. Specifically:

  • No Browsing History Tracking: We never log, track, or inspect the URLs, domains, or webpages you visit.
  • No Search Query Interception: Search queries typed into the omnibox or web engines are sent exclusively to your selected search provider (e.g., Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing) via encrypted HTTPS, never through intermediate logging proxies.
  • No Telemetry Beacons or Tracking Daemons: The browser contains zero background telemetry agents, pingback daemons, or analytical probes reporting your device uptime or behavioral telemetry.
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate commercial trackers or telemetry SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel, or Facebook Pixel) into any Windows, macOS, or Android builds.
  • No Device Fingerprinting: We do not construct hardware fingerprints, advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA), or persistent tracking profiles.

2. 100% On-Device Local Storage

All operational browser state—including Bookmarks, Browsing History, Session Cookies, Cache, Downloads List, and User Preferences—is stored exclusively and securely on your local device:

  • On Windows: Files reside in your protected user profile directory (%APPDATA%\RuhiBrowser or within the isolated Microsoft Store MSIX app-container sandbox).
  • On macOS: Files reside in your sandboxed application support directory (~/Library/Application Support/RuhiBrowser/ and ~/Library/Caches/RuhiBrowser/), isolated from other applications in compliance with macOS App Sandbox security policies.
  • On Android: Files reside in internal, OS-protected private storage (/data/data/com.ruhi.browser/), inaccessible to any third-party apps on the device.

This data belongs entirely to you. When you clear your browser history or uninstall the application, all local files are completely deleted from your storage drive.

3. Hardware-Grade Password & Credential Security

When you choose to save login credentials or form autofill in RUHI Browser:

  • On Windows: Credentials are cryptographically encrypted using the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) tied to your hardware TPM (Trusted Platform Module) and active Windows login profile.
  • On macOS: Passwords and security tokens are stored inside the encrypted Apple Keychain Services backed by the Apple Secure Enclave on Apple Silicon and Intel T2 chips, with optional Touch ID biometric unlock.
  • On Android: Security is enforced via the Android Keystore system with hardware-backed Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) and Knox isolation.

RUHI Applications maintains zero encryption keys, zero master passwords, and zero technical ability to inspect or recover your saved credentials.

4. Optional End-to-End Cloud Sync (Bookmarks & Tabs)

RUHI Browser offers an optional cloud synchronization capability powered by a secure Supabase backend:

  • Strictly Opt-In: Cloud sync is entirely optional. By default, RUHI Browser functions completely offline with zero network account requirements.
  • Encrypted Transport: When you voluntarily register or log into your RUHI Sync account, your selected bookmarks and active tabs are synchronized via secure, encrypted HTTPS protocols.
  • Isolated User Partitioning: Data is strictly linked to your authenticated account ID using Row-Level Security (RLS). No third-party data brokers or marketing engines have access.
  • Complete User Control: You can purge your synced cloud records or delete your synchronization profile at any time directly through Settings.

5. Integrated AdBlocker & Network Filtering

RUHI Browser features a client-side AdBlock engine engineered to block intrusive banner ads, video interruptions, popups, and malicious tracking scripts:

  • On macOS: Utilizes native WebKit ContentRuleList API. Blocking filters are compiled down to optimized bytecode executed directly inside the WebKit engine at C++ speed without JavaScript injection overhead.
  • On Windows & Android: Utilizes native network request filtering and domain-level interception before trackers can establish an outbound connection.
  • Zero Data Uploads: Block lists and filter rules are processed entirely on your local CPU. No log of visited websites or blocked advertisements is ever uploaded.

6. Web Permissions (Camera, Microphone, Location)

When a website requests access to your device hardware (camera, microphone, geolocation, or notifications):

  • Hardware permissions are never granted automatically. A prompt clearly requests your explicit confirmation.
  • RUHI Browser never records, taps, or stores audio, video streams, or location coordinates.
  • You can inspect, modify, or revoke permissions at any moment via the address bar lock icon or Browser Settings.

7. InPrivate / Incognito Browsing

When you open an InPrivate / Incognito session:

  • Browsing history, search queries, form entries, and downloaded file histories are not committed to disk.
  • Session cookies, local storage entries, and web caches are maintained exclusively in volatile RAM and are permanently wiped immediately upon closing the private window.

8. Optional AI Assistant Queries

If you voluntarily activate RUHI AI features (such as webpage summaries, text rewrites, or translations):

  • Your requested prompt is transmitted directly via encrypted HTTPS to the configured AI inference API endpoint solely to generate the immediate response.
  • RUHI Applications does not log, store, sell, or train foundational models on your submitted prompts.

9. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA Compliance)

RUHI Browser is a general-purpose web browsing tool. We do not knowingly solicit, harvest, or retain personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because RUHI Browser operates under a strict Zero-Telemetry framework, no personal information from any user, child or adult, is gathered or processed.

10. Global Regulatory Compliance

Our architecture and operational practices adhere to the highest international data privacy frameworks:

  • European Union GDPR: We collect zero personal data, fully satisfying the principles of Data Minimization (Article 5) and Privacy by Design and by Default (Article 25).
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell, share, or monetize consumer personal information.
  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines: Full compliance with Section 5.1 (Privacy and Data Use) ensuring total transparency and user data protection on macOS.
  • Microsoft Store Policy: Compliance with Section 10.5.1 regarding personal data protection and user privacy.
  • Google Play Store Developer Policy: Strict adherence to User Data policies and minimal privilege principles on Android.

11. User Control & Instant Data Erasure

You retain full control over your digital footprint. At any time, you can purge your browsing history, cookies, cache, and passwords:

  • Windows: Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete or go to Settings → Clear Browsing Data.
  • macOS: Press Cmd + Shift + Delete or go to RUHI Browser → Clear Browsing Data.
  • Android: Tap the menu button → Settings → Privacy → Clear Data.

12. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect architectural enhancements or international regulatory updates. Any changes will be published directly to this page with an updated "Effective Date".
